# Advertiser Guide — Seeker Quest League

Reach **Solana Mobile Seeker holders** through pay-per-view in-app ads.

---

## 🎯 Who you reach

- **100% Web3-native audience** — every player owns a Seeker phone
- Active daily — players open the app for the daily SOL tournament
- Crypto-literate — wallets connected, used to on-chain actions
- Premium segment of Solana mobile users

---

## 📊 Ad formats

| Format | Min view time | Best for |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Video** (up to 60s) | 15s | Brand awareness, NFT mint announcements, project explainers |
| **Image** (single) | 10s | Limited-time offers, drop announcements |
| **External link** | 5s | Direct CTA — open your site, mint page, X profile |

All formats include:
- Sponsor logo on the card
- Brand-colored accent
- One CTA button with custom label & URL

## 📐 Creative specs

### Video
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920 (portrait, 9:16) recommended; 16:9 also works
- **Duration:** 10-60 seconds
- **File:** MP4 (H.264) or WebM
- **Max size:** 30 MB
- **Audio:** optional (muted by default in feed)

### Image
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920 (portrait) or 1920×1080 (landscape)
- **File:** PNG or JPG
- **Max size:** 3 MB
- **Avoid:** dense text, fake CTAs, deceptive UI mimicry

### Thumbnail (card preview)
- **Resolution:** 600×600 minimum
- **File:** PNG or JPG
- **Shows in the card list** before tap

### Sponsor logo
- **Resolution:** 256×256 minimum, square
- **File:** PNG (transparent background preferred)

### Brand color
- **One hex color** (e.g. `#00FFAA`) — used for borders, buttons, accents

---

## ✍️ Copy & metadata

You provide:
- **Title** (max 60 chars) — shown big in the card and viewer
- **Description** (max 200 chars) — shown under title
- **Sponsor name** (max 30 chars)
- **Advertiser email** (for our records, not shown to players)
- **Advertiser website** (optional, public)
- **CTA label** (e.g. "Mint NFT", "Trade now", "Visit") — max 20 chars
- **CTA URL** (your landing page, mint page, X profile, etc.)

## 🚫 What we don't accept

- ❌ Gambling sites without proper licensing
- ❌ Tokens with no published smart contract
- ❌ Adult / NSFW content
- ❌ Misleading "claim airdrop" wallet-drainer pages
- ❌ Direct competitor games (we may approve adjacent games case-by-case)
- ❌ Anything illegal in your operating jurisdiction

We reserve the right to pause or reject any campaign at any time without
refund if it violates community guidelines.

## 📋 How to submit a campaign

### Step 1 — Send a proposal

Email **ads@seekerquest-league.com** with subject: `[CAMPAIGN] <your project name>`

Include in the body:
1. Project name + 1-2 sentence pitch
2. Desired tier (Starter / Growth / Featured)
3. Desired start date
4. Your X/Twitter or Telegram for callback
5. Your Solana wallet address (you'll pay from this)

### Step 2 — We reply within 24h

We confirm fit, tier, total budget, and per-view ORB.
You receive an invoice with our treasury address.

### Step 3 — Send creative materials

Use **WeTransfer**, **Google Drive**, or **Dropbox** to share:
- 1× video or image (per specs above)
- 1× thumbnail
- 1× sponsor logo
- 1× metadata text (title, description, CTA)

Or attach directly to email if total < 25 MB.

### Step 4 — Send payment

Transfer the agreed SOL amount to our campaign treasury (we send you the address).
Once confirmed on-chain (1-2 minutes), we upload your assets and publish.

### Step 5 — Campaign goes live

Usually within 2-4 hours of payment confirmation. You get a confirmation email
with the campaign ID and live preview link.

### Step 6 — Daily report

We email you daily:
- Total views
- Total ORB paid out
- Click-through rate (if CTA URL provided)
- Demographics (.skr domain rate, Founder rate, day-1 retention)

## 🛡 Anti-fraud guarantees

| Mechanism | How it protects you |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **SGT verification** (planned) | Optional: only count views from verified Seeker owners |
| **Per-device cap** | Each device can claim your reward at most N times (you choose, default 1) |
| **Min view time** | Player must keep ad open ≥ your minimum seconds before claiming |
| **Daily total cap** | Per-user daily ORB cap from ads prevents farming |
| **Anti-bot** | Server-side RLS + UUID device tracking |
| **Refund policy** | If we detect fraud > 10% of your spend, we refund the excess |
