Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026  ·  Effective Date: May 29, 2026

Welcome to Seeker Quest League ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website located at seekerquest-league.com (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you register.
  • Blockchain / Wallet Data: Your public Solana wallet address used to claim OIB tokens and SOL rewards. We never collect private keys.
  • Usage Data: In-game activity, tap counts, quest completions, league rank, session duration, and device information (OS, model, app version).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, referring URLs, and cookies or similar tracking technologies.
  • Communications: Messages you send us via email or support channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Process and distribute OIB token rewards and SOL prizes;
  • Manage leaderboards, seasons, and league rankings;
  • Send transactional emails (account verification, reward notifications);
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent);
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or abuse;
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Blockchain & On-Chain Data

All on-chain transactions (token transfers, reward distributions) are recorded permanently on the Solana blockchain and are publicly visible. We have no control over this public data. Please ensure your wallet address does not contain personally identifiable information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, email delivery, and payment processors acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in across sessions;
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve the Service;
  • Remember your preferences.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at privacy@seekerquest-league.com. Note: on-chain transaction records cannot be deleted.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption, secure password hashing, and access controls. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. Use the Service at your own risk.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Object to or restrict processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing).

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@seekerquest-league.com.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Raydium, Jupiter, social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Support: support@seekerquest-league.com
